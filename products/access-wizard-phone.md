Your phone is the key.

Connect it to a gate or an electric strike door. It replaces the key or fob with your phone. No cloud account.

## What you get

- **No subscription.** All features are free forever.
- **Phone is the key.** Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. No app to install.
- **Guest invites.** Send a Bluetooth invite. They tap Open. It expires, or you cancel it.
- **Schedule.** Set open and closed hours.
- **Gate controllers and electric strikes.** Highly compatible with both.
- **Low power mode.** Made for solar setups. Uses 3 watt-hours per day of your solar budget.
- **Remote reporting.** Send the history to your own web address.
- **A record.** See who opened it and when.
- **Home Wi-Fi.** Join your home network if you like.
- **Reach it from anywhere** through your own VPN. VPNs keep their security current far better than a small device can, and your AI can help you set one up.
- **Full AI compatibility.** Give your AI the unit's manual and settings. Ask it to set the unit up for you.
