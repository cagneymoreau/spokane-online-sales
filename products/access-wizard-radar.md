Vehicle detection with no loop to bury. Phone access, plus a radar that sees a vehicle at the gate, moving or stopped. No pavement cut. Your phone is still the key. No cloud account.

The first picture is an illustration of the production unit: the outdoor junction box on a post beside the drive. The photos after it are field test units on our own driveway and street, in the test enclosure.

## What you get

- **No subscription.** All features are free forever.
- **Knows when a vehicle is there,** moving or stopped. Nothing to bury.
- **Four ways to mount it.** Straight across one lane or two, angled at one lane to tell in from out, or overhead looking down.
- **Counts vehicles.** Two-lane and angled single-lane mounts also count in and out.
- **You set the zone,** from 3 ft to 31 ft. Nothing outside it opens the gate.
- **Holds for a parked vehicle,** up to two hours. You can change it.
- **Follows rain, snow and a wet drive,** so a change in the weather is not a vehicle.
- **Phone is the key.** Wi-Fi or Bluetooth. Guest invites. Schedule open and closed hours.
- **Gate controllers and electric strikes.** Highly compatible with both.
- **Reach it from anywhere** through your own VPN, and send events to your own web address.
- **Full AI compatibility.** Give your AI the unit's manual and settings. Ask it to set the unit up for you.

**Not a safety device.** The radar is for convenience. It must not be used as a safety, reversing or obstruction sensor. The gate keeps its own photo eyes and edge sensors. Read Safety first before you install.

Everything else works like [Access Wizard Phone](/products/access-wizard-phone/).
